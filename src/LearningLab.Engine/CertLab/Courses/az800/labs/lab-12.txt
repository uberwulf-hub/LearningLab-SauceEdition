# Lab: lab-12 | DHCP for a new site, and a second server | u12 | 40
Harborline is opening a new depot network (10.30.0.0/24, reached through a relay on its router) and wants head office DHCP to survive the loss of SRV1. CORE1, the Server Core machine, will be the standby DHCP server.

@vars
site = Warehouse | Yard | Depot
days = 2 | 3 | 5
res = 150-170
@endvars

@task t01 | Networking | 25 | SRV1
On SRV1, create the scope **{site} devices** for 10.30.0.50 to 10.30.0.250 (mask 255.255.255.0) with a lease of **{days} days**. Keep 10.30.0.50-10.30.0.59 free for the depot's switches and printers, and give the scope the router 10.30.0.1.
@check admin | the scope, its lease and its exclusion
dhcp(SRV1).scope["10.30.0.0"].name == "{site} devices"
dhcp(SRV1).scope["10.30.0.0"].leasedays == {days}
dhcp(SRV1).scope["10.30.0.0"].exclusions has 10.30.0.50-10.30.0.59
@check admin | the router option is set on the scope
dhcp(SRV1).scope["10.30.0.0"].option["3"] == 10.30.0.1
@hint Add-DhcpServerv4Scope -LeaseDuration {days}.00:00:00, Add-DhcpServerv4ExclusionRange, Set-DhcpServerv4OptionValue -ScopeId 10.30.0.0 -Router.
@solution SRV1
Add-DhcpServerv4Scope -Name "{site} devices" -StartRange 10.30.0.50 -EndRange 10.30.0.250 -SubnetMask 255.255.255.0 -LeaseDuration {days}.00:00:00
Add-DhcpServerv4ExclusionRange -ScopeId 10.30.0.0 -StartRange 10.30.0.50 -EndRange 10.30.0.59
Set-DhcpServerv4OptionValue -ScopeId 10.30.0.0 -Router 10.30.0.1
@explain
The DNS servers come from the server-level option 006, so the scope only needs its own router. Clients in 10.30.0.0/24 reach SRV1 through the relay, which tells SRV1 which scope to use.
@endtask

@task t02 | Networking | 20 | SRV1
The head office printer **PRN-HQ-01** (MAC address 00-1B-A9-4C-21-07) must always get **10.10.0.{res}**. Reserve it.
@check admin | the reservation matches the printer's MAC address
dhcp(SRV1).scope["10.10.0.0"].reservation["10.10.0.{res}"].mac == 00-1B-A9-4C-21-07
@hint Add-DhcpServerv4Reservation -ScopeId 10.10.0.0 -IPAddress -ClientId.
@solution SRV1
Add-DhcpServerv4Reservation -ScopeId 10.10.0.0 -IPAddress 10.10.0.{res} -ClientId 00-1B-A9-4C-21-07 -Name PRN-HQ-01
@explain
The printer moves to the reserved address the next time it renews its lease. A reservation keeps the printer on DHCP, so it still gets the router and DNS options.
@endtask

@task t03 | Networking | 30 | CORE1
Install the DHCP Server role on **CORE1** and authorize it. Then, on SRV1, protect the scope 10.10.0.0 with a **hot standby** failover relationship called **HQ-Standby**: SRV1 active, CORE1 standby, a reserve of **10%**, an MCLT of **2 hours** and the shared secret `St4ndby#26`.
@check admin | CORE1 is an authorized DHCP server
dhcp(CORE1).authorized == Yes
@check admin | the hot standby relationship
dhcp(SRV1).failover["HQ-Standby"].mode == HotStandby
dhcp(SRV1).failover["HQ-Standby"].reserve == 10
dhcp(SRV1).failover["HQ-Standby"].mclt == 120
@check admin | CORE1 has the scope with the printer's reservation
dhcp(CORE1).scope["10.10.0.0"].reservation["10.10.0.{res}"].mac == 00-1B-A9-4C-21-07
@hint Install-WindowsFeature DHCP and Add-DhcpServerInDC on CORE1; on SRV1 Add-DhcpServerv4Failover -ServerRole Active -ReservePercent 10 -MaxClientLeadTime 02:00:00.
@solution CORE1
Install-WindowsFeature DHCP -IncludeManagementTools
Add-DhcpServerInDC -DnsName core1.corp.example -IPAddress 10.10.0.40
@solution SRV1
Add-DhcpServerv4Failover -Name HQ-Standby -ScopeId 10.10.0.0 -PartnerServer CORE1 -ServerRole Active -ReservePercent 10 -MaxClientLeadTime 02:00:00 -SharedSecret 'St4ndby#26'
@explain
-ServerRole and -ReservePercent make the relationship hot standby. Creating it copied the whole scope, reservations included, to CORE1.
@endtask

@task t04 | Networking | 25 | SRV1
New cameras will use 10.10.0.190-10.10.0.199 with fixed addresses. Exclude that range from the HQ scope on SRV1 and make sure CORE1 knows about it too.
@check admin | both partners exclude the cameras' range
dhcp(SRV1).scope["10.10.0.0"].exclusions has 10.10.0.190-10.10.0.199
dhcp(CORE1).scope["10.10.0.0"].exclusions has 10.10.0.190-10.10.0.199
@hint Add the exclusion on SRV1, then Invoke-DhcpServerv4FailoverReplication.
@solution SRV1
Add-DhcpServerv4ExclusionRange -ScopeId 10.10.0.0 -StartRange 10.10.0.190 -EndRange 10.10.0.199
Invoke-DhcpServerv4FailoverReplication -Name HQ-Standby -Force
@explain
Failover partners share leases automatically, but not configuration. Without the replication CORE1 could hand a camera's address to a PC while SRV1 is down.
@endtask
