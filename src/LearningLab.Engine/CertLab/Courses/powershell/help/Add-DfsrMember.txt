SYNOPSIS: Adds servers to a DFS Replication group.
DESCRIPTION:
Add-DfsrMember makes servers members of a group. Each needs the DFS Replication role service. Members don't replicate yet: they also need connections (Add-DfsrConnection) and a content path (Set-DfsrMembership).
PARAMETER GroupName:
The replication group. Position 0.
PARAMETER ComputerName:
The server or servers. Position 1.
PARAMETER Description:
A description.
EXAMPLE: Add-DfsrMember -GroupName Sales-Replication -ComputerName FS1, SRV1
Step 3: both servers join the group.
EXAMPLE: Get-DfsrMember -GroupName Sales-Replication
Checks the members.
RELATED: Get-DfsrMember, Add-DfsrConnection, Set-DfsrMembership, New-DfsReplicationGroup
