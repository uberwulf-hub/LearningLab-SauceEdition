SYNOPSIS: Pauses a workflow job so it can be resumed later.
DESCRIPTION:
Suspend-Job pauses a job that supports it: in Windows PowerShell 5.1 those are workflow jobs (started from a workflow with -AsJob). The job keeps its state and can be continued with Resume-Job.

Ordinary background jobs from Start-Job and remote jobs from Invoke-Command -AsJob cannot be suspended; for them Suspend-Job reports an error. Use Stop-Job to end such a job instead.
PARAMETER Id:
The job's Id number.
PARAMETER Name:
The job's name. Wildcards are allowed.
PARAMETER Job:
The job objects, for example from Get-Job.
PARAMETER Force:
Suspends a workflow job right away instead of at its next checkpoint.
PARAMETER Wait:
Waits until the job is suspended before returning.
EXAMPLE: Get-Job -Name Nightly | Suspend-Job
Pauses the workflow job named Nightly.
NOTES:
Workflows are not part of PowerShell 7, so Suspend-Job and Resume-Job have little use there. The Cert Lab practice console does not simulate workflows, so this command always reports that the job cannot be suspended.
