SYNOPSIS: Gets organizational units (OUs) from Active Directory.
DESCRIPTION:
OUs are the folders of a domain: they hold users, computers, groups and other OUs, and they are where you link Group Policy and delegate administration.
Get-ADOrganizationalUnit returns OUs with Name, DistinguishedName and the linked GPOs (LinkedGroupPolicyObjects). Use -Identity with a distinguished name, or -Filter.
PARAMETER Identity:
One OU, as a distinguished name such as "OU=Sales,OU=Staff,DC=corp,DC=example" or its GUID. Position 0, and takes pipeline input.
PARAMETER Filter:
A search in the PowerShell filter language, such as "Name -eq 'Sales'". Use * for all.
PARAMETER Properties:
Extra properties, such as ProtectedFromAccidentalDeletion and Description.
PARAMETER SearchBase:
Only searches below this container.
PARAMETER SearchScope:
Base, OneLevel or Subtree (the default).
PARAMETER Server:
The domain controller to ask.
EXAMPLE: Get-ADOrganizationalUnit -Filter * | Select-Object Name, DistinguishedName
Every OU in corp.example.
EXAMPLE: Get-ADOrganizationalUnit -Filter * -SearchBase "OU=Staff,DC=corp,DC=example" -SearchScope OneLevel
The department OUs directly under Staff.
RELATED: New-ADOrganizationalUnit, Set-ADOrganizationalUnit, Remove-ADOrganizationalUnit, Get-GPInheritance
