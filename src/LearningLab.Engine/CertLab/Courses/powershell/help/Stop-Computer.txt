SYNOPSIS: Shuts the computer down.
DESCRIPTION:
Stop-Computer turns Windows off. A real computer then needs someone to press its power button; the practice machines start again at once, so you can go on.
PARAMETER ComputerName:
The computers to shut down. Position 0.
PARAMETER Force:
Shuts down even if other users are signed in.
EXAMPLE: Stop-Computer -ComputerName WIN-CL1 -Force
RELATED: Restart-Computer
