SYNOPSIS: Waits for a number of seconds or milliseconds.
DESCRIPTION:
Start-Sleep pauses the script. Use it between retries, or to give a service time to start.

In Cert Lab's practice machines, Start-Sleep moves the machines' simulated clock on at once instead of really waiting, so scheduled tasks and jobs that are due on the way run.
PARAMETER Seconds:
How many seconds to wait (the first positional parameter; alias s).
PARAMETER Milliseconds:
How many milliseconds to wait (alias ms).
EXAMPLE: Start-Sleep -Seconds 5
Waits five seconds.
EXAMPLE: Start-Sleep 2; Get-Date
Waits two seconds, then shows the time.
NOTES:
sleep is an alias of Start-Sleep.
RELATED: Wait-Job, Get-Date
