SYNOPSIS: Shows who has which permissions on a Group Policy Object.
DESCRIPTION:
GPO permissions decide two things: who may edit the GPO, and who it applies to. A GPO applies only to users and computers that have both Read and Apply Group Policy (the GpoApply level); by default that is Authenticated Users.
Levels: GpoRead (read only), GpoApply (read and apply), GpoEdit (edit settings), GpoEditDeleteModifySecurity (full control) and GpoCustom.
PARAMETER Name:
The GPO's display name. Position 0.
PARAMETER Guid:
The GPO, by GUID.
PARAMETER TargetName:
One user, group or computer to check.
PARAMETER TargetType:
What TargetName is: User, Group, Computer or WellKnownGroup.
PARAMETER All:
Lists every permission entry.
PARAMETER Domain:
The domain, if not your own.
PARAMETER Server:
The domain controller to ask.
EXAMPLE: Get-GPPermission "Sales Desktop" -All | Select-Object Trustee, Permission
Everyone with rights on the GPO.
EXAMPLE: Get-GPPermission "Sales Desktop" -TargetName Sales -TargetType Group
Only the Sales group's permission.
RELATED: Set-GPPermission, Get-GPO
