SYNOPSIS: Shows the settings of a Hyper-V host.
DESCRIPTION:
Get-VMHost shows the host itself: its LogicalProcessorCount, MemoryCapacity, the default folders for new VMs (VirtualMachinePath) and their disks (VirtualHardDiskPath), whether enhanced session mode is allowed and whether NUMA spanning is on.

The default folders are on drive C:, which is why many admins move them to a data drive with Set-VMHost.
PARAMETER ComputerName:
The Hyper-V host to ask.
EXAMPLE: Get-VMHost -ComputerName HV1
HV1 has 16 logical processors and 64 GB of memory.
EXAMPLE: Get-VMHost | Format-List VirtualMachinePath, VirtualHardDiskPath
Where new VMs and disks go.
RELATED: Set-VMHost, Get-VM, Get-VMSwitch
