SYNOPSIS: Lists the remoting endpoints registered on this computer.
DESCRIPTION:
Get-PSSessionConfiguration shows each endpoint with its name, PowerShell version, who may connect (Permission) and, for JEA endpoints, its session type, roles and settings file.

Windows has built-in endpoints: microsoft.powershell (the one used when you do not name one), microsoft.powershell32 and microsoft.powershell.workflow. Endpoints you register appear next to them.

Remoting must be on (WinRM listening), as on WIN-SRV1; on WIN-CL1 the command fails until remoting is enabled.
PARAMETER Name:
The endpoint name. Wildcards work.
EXAMPLE: Get-PSSessionConfiguration
Lists every endpoint with its permissions.
EXAMPLE: Get-PSSessionConfiguration -Name HelpDesk | Select-Object Name, SessionType, RunAsVirtualAccount, RoleDefinitions
Shows the main settings of a JEA endpoint.
NOTES:
On a real PC this needs PowerShell run as administrator.
RELATED: Register-PSSessionConfiguration, Unregister-PSSessionConfiguration, Get-PSSessionCapability, Enable-PSRemoting
