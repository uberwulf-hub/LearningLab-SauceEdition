SYNOPSIS: Starts a Microsoft Defender Antivirus scan.
DESCRIPTION:
Start-MpScan runs a quick scan (the places malware usually hides) or a full scan (every file). Intune's Quick scan and Full scan remote actions do the same from the portal.
PARAMETER ScanType:
QuickScan (the default), FullScan or CustomScan.
PARAMETER ScanPath:
The folder to scan with CustomScan.
EXAMPLE: Start-MpScan -ScanType QuickScan
A quick scan.
RELATED: Get-MpComputerStatus, Update-MpSignature
