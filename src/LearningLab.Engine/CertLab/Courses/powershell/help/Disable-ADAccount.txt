SYNOPSIS: Turns off a user, computer or service account.
DESCRIPTION:
Disable-ADAccount sets the "account is disabled" flag. The account keeps its SID, groups and permissions but can't sign in. It's the usual first step when someone leaves: disable now, delete later.
Sessions that are already signed in keep working until their Kerberos tickets run out.
PARAMETER Identity:
The account: a user, a computer (CL1$) or a service account. Position 0, and takes pipeline input.
PARAMETER PassThru:
Returns the account after the change.
EXAMPLE: Disable-ADAccount dpatel
Turns off Dev Patel's account.
EXAMPLE: Get-ADUser -Filter "Department -eq 'HR'" | Disable-ADAccount -WhatIf
Shows which HR accounts would be disabled, without changing anything.
RELATED: Enable-ADAccount, Search-ADAccount, Remove-ADUser
