SYNOPSIS: Ends a DHCP failover relationship.
DESCRIPTION:
Remove-DhcpServerv4Failover deletes the relationship. The scopes stay on this server; on the partner they are removed. Clients that leased from the partner will move to this server when they renew.
PARAMETER Name:
The relationship or relationships to end.
PARAMETER Force:
Removes without asking.
EXAMPLE: Remove-DhcpServerv4Failover -Name HQ-Failover -ComputerName SRV1
SRV1 serves the HQ scope alone again.
RELATED: Get-DhcpServerv4Failover, Add-DhcpServerv4Failover
