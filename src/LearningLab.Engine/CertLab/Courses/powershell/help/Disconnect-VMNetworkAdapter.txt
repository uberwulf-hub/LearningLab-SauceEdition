SYNOPSIS: Unplugs a VM's network adapter from its switch.
DESCRIPTION:
Disconnect-VMNetworkAdapter leaves the adapter in the VM but connected to nothing, like pulling the cable. Handy to isolate an infected or misbehaving VM quickly while keeping it running.
PARAMETER VMName:
The VM or VMs. Position 0.
PARAMETER Name:
Only this adapter.
EXAMPLE: Disconnect-VMNetworkAdapter -VMName APP-OLD -ComputerName HV1
APP-OLD is cut off from the network.
EXAMPLE: Connect-VMNetworkAdapter -VMName APP-OLD -SwitchName LAN
Plugs it back in.
RELATED: Connect-VMNetworkAdapter, Get-VMNetworkAdapter
