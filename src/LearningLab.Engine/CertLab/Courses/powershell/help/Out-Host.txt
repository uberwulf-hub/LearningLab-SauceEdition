SYNOPSIS: Sends output straight to the screen.
DESCRIPTION:
Out-Host formats objects and writes them to the console. It is the last step of Out-Default, so most of the time you do not need it.

It is useful when you want something shown on screen even though the rest of the output is being captured or passed on, for example inside a function whose output goes into a variable. Out-Host's text cannot be captured: it goes to the screen only.

On a real console, -Paging shows long output one screen at a time (press Space for the next page, Enter for the next line, Q to stop).
PARAMETER InputObject:
What to show. Usually it comes through the pipeline.
PARAMETER Paging:
Shows the output one page at a time on a real console.
EXAMPLE: 1..3 | Out-Host
Shows 1, 2 and 3 on screen.
EXAMPLE: $x = 1..3 | Out-Host; $null -eq $x
The numbers are shown, but nothing reaches $x: True.
EXAMPLE: Import-Csv C:\LabFiles\employees.csv | Out-Host -Paging
On a real console, shows the employees a screenful at a time.
NOTES:
The alias oh exists in 5.1.

The more function (and help, which uses it) is the other way to page through long output.

-Paging does not work in the PowerShell ISE or in Visual Studio Code's integrated console.
RELATED: Out-Default, Out-String, Write-Host, Out-Null
