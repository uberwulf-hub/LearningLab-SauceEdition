SYNOPSIS: Deletes a classic log you created, or unregisters a source (Windows PowerShell 5.1 only).
DESCRIPTION:
With -LogName, Remove-EventLog deletes the log and its sources. With -Source, it only unregisters the source. Windows' own logs cannot be removed.
PARAMETER LogName:
The logs to delete. Position 0.
PARAMETER Source:
The sources to unregister.
EXAMPLE: Remove-EventLog -Source LabApp
Unregisters a source.
EXAMPLE: Remove-EventLog -LogName LabLog
Deletes a log you created.
RELATED: New-EventLog, Clear-EventLog
