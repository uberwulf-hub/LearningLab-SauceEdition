SYNOPSIS: Turns on an Active Directory optional feature, such as the Recycle Bin.
DESCRIPTION:
Enable-ADOptionalFeature switches on a forest-wide feature. For the Recycle Bin the scope is ForestOrConfigurationSet and the target is the forest; it needs the Windows Server 2008 R2 forest functional level and Enterprise Admins rights.
Turning the Recycle Bin on can't be undone. Objects deleted before it was on can't be restored with their attributes.
PARAMETER Identity:
The feature, such as "Recycle Bin Feature". Position 0.
PARAMETER Scope:
ForestOrConfigurationSet or Domain. Position 1.
PARAMETER Target:
The forest or domain to enable it in, such as corp.example. Position 2.
PARAMETER Server:
The domain controller to make the change on.
EXAMPLE: Enable-ADOptionalFeature "Recycle Bin Feature" -Scope ForestOrConfigurationSet -Target corp.example
Turns on the AD Recycle Bin for the whole forest.
EXAMPLE: Get-ADOptionalFeature "Recycle Bin Feature" | Select-Object Name, EnabledScopes
Checks that it is on.
RELATED: Get-ADOptionalFeature, Restore-ADObject, Get-ADObject
