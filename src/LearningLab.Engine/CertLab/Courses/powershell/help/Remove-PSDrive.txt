SYNOPSIS: Removes a drive made with New-PSDrive.
DESCRIPTION:
Remove-PSDrive takes a drive name away. The items it pointed at are not touched. You cannot remove the drive you are standing in; move to another drive first.
PARAMETER Name:
The drive to remove (wildcards allowed).
PARAMETER LiteralName:
The drive to remove, name taken exactly.
PARAMETER PSProvider:
Only drives of this provider.
PARAMETER Force:
Removes it even if it is the current drive.
EXAMPLE: Remove-PSDrive Lab
Removes the Lab: drive.
RELATED: New-PSDrive, Get-PSDrive
