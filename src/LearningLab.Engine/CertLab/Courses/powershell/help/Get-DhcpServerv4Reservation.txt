SYNOPSIS: Lists the address reservations of a DHCP server.
DESCRIPTION:
Get-DhcpServerv4Reservation shows each reservation with its IPAddress, ClientId (MAC address), Name and Type. Filter by scope, address or MAC address.
PARAMETER ScopeId:
Shows the reservations of this scope.
PARAMETER IPAddress:
Shows the reservation for this address.
PARAMETER ClientId:
Shows the reservation for this MAC address.
EXAMPLE: Get-DhcpServerv4Reservation -ScopeId 10.10.0.0 -ComputerName SRV1
Every reservation in the HQ scope.
EXAMPLE: Get-DhcpServerv4Reservation -IPAddress 10.10.0.150
Who has 10.10.0.150 reserved.
RELATED: Add-DhcpServerv4Reservation, Remove-DhcpServerv4Reservation, Get-DhcpServerv4Lease
