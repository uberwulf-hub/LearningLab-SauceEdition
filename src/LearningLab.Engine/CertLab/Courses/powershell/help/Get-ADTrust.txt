SYNOPSIS: Lists the trusts between this domain and other domains or forests.
DESCRIPTION:
A trust lets accounts of one domain use resources in another. Get-ADTrust shows each trust's partner (Target), Direction (Inbound, Outbound or BiDirectional), whether it is transitive, and its type (forest, external, realm...).
Domains in the same forest trust each other automatically (two-way, transitive). Trusts to other forests are created on purpose, for example after a merger. The direction can confuse: the trusting domain holds the resources, and the trusted domain holds the accounts.
PARAMETER Identity:
One trust, by the partner's name. Position 0.
PARAMETER Filter:
A search, such as *.
PARAMETER Server:
The domain controller to ask.
EXAMPLE: Get-ADTrust -Filter * | Select-Object Name, Direction, ForestTransitive, IntraForest
Every trust of the domain.
EXAMPLE: Get-ADTrust -Filter "Direction -eq 'BiDirectional'"
Only the two-way trusts.
RELATED: Get-ADDomain, Get-ADForest
