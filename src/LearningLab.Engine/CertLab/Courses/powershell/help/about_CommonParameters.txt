SHORT DESCRIPTION
    The parameters every cmdlet and advanced function has.

LONG DESCRIPTION
    -Verbose (-vb)            show the command's verbose messages
    -Debug (-db)              show its debug messages
    -ErrorAction (-ea)        what to do with its non-terminating errors:
                              Continue, SilentlyContinue, Ignore, Stop, Inquire
    -ErrorVariable (-ev)      also store its errors in this variable (name
                              without $; +name adds to it)
    -WarningAction (-wa)      what to do with its warnings
    -WarningVariable (-wv)    store its warnings in a variable
    -InformationAction (-infa) what to do with its information messages
    -InformationVariable (-iv) store its information messages in a variable
    -OutVariable (-ov)        also store its output in a variable
    -OutBuffer (-ob)          how many objects to collect before passing them on
    -PipelineVariable (-pv)   keep its current object in a variable for later
                              commands of the same pipeline

    Commands that change things also have:

    -WhatIf (-wi)             only say what would be done
    -Confirm (-cf)            ask before each change

    Your own functions get these when they use [CmdletBinding()]
    (and [CmdletBinding(SupportsShouldProcess)] for -WhatIf and -Confirm).
