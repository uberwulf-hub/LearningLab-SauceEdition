SYNOPSIS: Removes registry-based policy settings from a Group Policy Object.
DESCRIPTION:
Remove-GPRegistryValue deletes values (or, without -ValueName, every value under the key) from a GPO. For real policy settings under a Policies key, computers and users drop the setting at their next refresh.
PARAMETER Name:
The GPO's display name. Position 0.
PARAMETER Guid:
The GPO, by GUID.
PARAMETER Key:
The registry key, starting with HKLM\ or HKCU\.
PARAMETER ValueName:
The values to remove.
PARAMETER Domain:
The domain, if not your own.
PARAMETER Server:
The domain controller to make the change on.
EXAMPLE: Remove-GPRegistryValue "Sales Desktop" -Key "HKCU\Software\Policies\Microsoft\Windows\Control Panel\Desktop" -ValueName ScreenSaveTimeOut
Removes the screen saver setting from the GPO.
EXAMPLE: Remove-GPRegistryValue "Sales Desktop" -Key "HKCU\Software\Policies\Microsoft\Windows\Control Panel\Desktop"
Removes every value the GPO sets under that key.
RELATED: Set-GPRegistryValue, Get-GPRegistryValue
