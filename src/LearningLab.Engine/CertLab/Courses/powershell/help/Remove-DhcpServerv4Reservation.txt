SYNOPSIS: Deletes address reservations.
DESCRIPTION:
Remove-DhcpServerv4Reservation deletes reservations by address, by MAC address or all of a scope. The device keeps its current lease; at the next renewal it may get a different address.
PARAMETER ScopeId:
The scope.
PARAMETER IPAddress:
The reserved address to free.
PARAMETER ClientId:
The MAC address whose reservation to delete.
EXAMPLE: Remove-DhcpServerv4Reservation -IPAddress 10.10.0.150 -ComputerName SRV1
Frees the printer's reserved address.
RELATED: Add-DhcpServerv4Reservation, Get-DhcpServerv4Reservation
