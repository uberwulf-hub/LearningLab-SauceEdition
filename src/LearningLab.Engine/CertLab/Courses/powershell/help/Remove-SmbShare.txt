SYNOPSIS: Stops sharing a folder.
DESCRIPTION:
Remove-SmbShare removes the share. The folder and its files stay on the disk; only network access through that name ends. Users who have it open lose their connection.

It asks for confirmation; -Force skips it.
PARAMETER Name:
The share or shares. Position 0.
PARAMETER Force:
Removes without asking.
EXAMPLE: Remove-SmbShare -Name Projects -Force
D:\Shares\Projects is no longer shared.
RELATED: Get-SmbShare, New-SmbShare
