SYNOPSIS: Deletes a zone from a DNS server.
DESCRIPTION:
Remove-DnsServerZone deletes the zone and every record in it. For an AD-integrated zone the deletion replicates, so the zone disappears from every DC in its replication scope, not just this one.

It asks for confirmation; -Force skips the question. There is no undo apart from restoring from backup (or the AD Recycle Bin for AD-integrated zones), so check the name twice.

It also removes stub zones, secondary zones and conditional forwarders.
PARAMETER Name:
The zone to delete.
PARAMETER Force:
Deletes without asking.
EXAMPLE: Remove-DnsServerZone -Name lab.example -ComputerName DC1
Deletes the zone after asking you to confirm.
EXAMPLE: Remove-DnsServerZone tidewater.example -Force
Removes the conditional forwarder (or stub, or secondary) for the partner domain without asking.
RELATED: Get-DnsServerZone, Add-DnsServerPrimaryZone
