SYNOPSIS: Turns a scheduled task off without deleting it.
DESCRIPTION:
A disabled task keeps its settings but its triggers do nothing and it cannot be started until it is enabled again. The command outputs the task.
PARAMETER TaskName:
The task. Position 0.
PARAMETER TaskPath:
Its folder. Position 1.
PARAMETER InputObject:
Task objects from the pipeline.
EXAMPLE: Disable-ScheduledTask -TaskName Nightly
EXAMPLE: Get-ScheduledTask -TaskPath \Lab\ | Disable-ScheduledTask
Disables every task in a folder.
RELATED: Enable-ScheduledTask, Unregister-ScheduledTask
