SYNOPSIS: Counts objects and works out the sum, average, smallest and largest values, or counts lines, words and characters.
DESCRIPTION:
Measure-Object answers "how many" and "how much" questions about what comes through the pipeline. With no options it just counts. Add -Sum, -Average, -Maximum and -Minimum to work out those values, either of the objects themselves (numbers) or of one property (-Property Salary).

For text, -Line, -Word and -Character count lines, words and characters.

The result is a single measurement object; read one value with dot notation, such as (... | Measure-Object -Sum).Sum.
PARAMETER Property:
The property to measure. Position 0.
PARAMETER Sum:
Adds the values together.
PARAMETER Average:
Works out the mean value.
PARAMETER Maximum:
Finds the largest value.
PARAMETER Minimum:
Finds the smallest value.
PARAMETER Line:
Counts lines of text.
PARAMETER Word:
Counts words (anything separated by spaces, tabs or new lines).
PARAMETER Character:
Counts characters.
PARAMETER IgnoreWhiteSpace:
With -Character, leaves spaces and tabs out of the count.
PARAMETER InputObject:
The objects to measure. Usually they come through the pipeline.
EXAMPLE: 1..10 | Measure-Object -Sum -Average -Maximum -Minimum
Count 10, Average 5.5, Sum 55, Maximum 10, Minimum 1.
EXAMPLE: Import-Csv C:\LabFiles\employees.csv | Measure-Object -Property Salary -Sum -Average -Maximum
Totals the salary column. The text values are read as numbers for the sum and average.
EXAMPLE: (Import-Csv C:\LabFiles\employees.csv | Measure-Object).Count
Just the number of employees: 10.
EXAMPLE: 'one two three', 'four five' | Measure-Object -Line -Word -Character
Two lines, five words, 22 characters.
NOTES:
The alias is measure.

-Sum and -Average need numbers. A value that cannot be read as a number stops Measure-Object with an error.

(... | Measure-Object).Count works even when there is only one object or none. In 5.1, an array's .Count also works on single objects, but Measure-Object makes it explicit.

PowerShell 7 adds -AllStats and -StandardDeviation, and allows -Property to be a script block or have wildcards.
RELATED: Group-Object, Sort-Object, Measure-Command, Select-Object
