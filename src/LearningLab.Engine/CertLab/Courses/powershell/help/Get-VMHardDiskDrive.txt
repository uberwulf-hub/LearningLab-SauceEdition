SYNOPSIS: Lists the virtual hard disks attached to a VM.
DESCRIPTION:
Get-VMHardDiskDrive shows each disk drive of a VM: ControllerType (IDE or SCSI), ControllerNumber, ControllerLocation and Path (the disk file). Pipe the result to Get-VHD for sizes and types.
PARAMETER VMName:
The VM or VMs. Position 0.
PARAMETER VM:
VM objects piped from Get-VM.
EXAMPLE: Get-VMHardDiskDrive -VMName APP-OLD -ComputerName HV1
APP-OLD boots from an IDE disk, as Generation 1 VMs do.
EXAMPLE: Get-VMHardDiskDrive -VMName APP-OLD | Get-VHD
The details of its disk file.
RELATED: Add-VMHardDiskDrive, Get-VHD, Get-VM
