SYNOPSIS: Lists the network adapters of virtual machines (or of the host).
DESCRIPTION:
Get-VMNetworkAdapter shows each virtual adapter with its VMName, SwitchName, MacAddress, Status and IPAddresses (the guest reports these through integration services).

-ManagementOS shows the host's own virtual adapters, the ones an External switch with AllowManagementOS creates. -All shows both.
PARAMETER VMName:
The VM or VMs. Position 0.
PARAMETER VM:
VM objects piped from Get-VM.
PARAMETER ManagementOS:
Shows the host's virtual adapters.
PARAMETER All:
Shows the adapters of every VM and of the host.
EXAMPLE: Get-VMNetworkAdapter -VMName APP-OLD -ComputerName HV1
Which switch APP-OLD uses and its MAC address.
EXAMPLE: Get-VMNetworkAdapter -ManagementOS
The host's own adapters on its switches.
RELATED: Add-VMNetworkAdapter, Set-VMNetworkAdapter, Connect-VMNetworkAdapter, Disconnect-VMNetworkAdapter, Get-VMSwitch
