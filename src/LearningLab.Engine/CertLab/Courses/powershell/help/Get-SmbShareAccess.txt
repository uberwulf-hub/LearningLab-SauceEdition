SYNOPSIS: Shows the share permissions of a share.
DESCRIPTION:
Get-SmbShareAccess lists the share permission entries: AccountName, AccessControlType (Allow or Deny) and AccessRight (Full, Change, Read or Custom).

These are only the share permissions; the NTFS permissions on the folder (Get-Acl) also count, and the more restrictive wins.
PARAMETER Name:
The share or shares. Position 0.
EXAMPLE: Get-SmbShareAccess -Name Sales
On FS1, Sales gives Everyone Full: the real control is in NTFS.
EXAMPLE: Get-SmbShareAccess -Name HR
CORP\HR has Change and the administrators Full.
RELATED: Grant-SmbShareAccess, Revoke-SmbShareAccess, Block-SmbShareAccess, Get-Acl
