SYNOPSIS: Lists the volumes with Data Deduplication and their savings.
DESCRIPTION:
Get-DedupVolume shows each volume with deduplication: Enabled, UsageType, SavedSpace, SavingsRate (percent) and the settings such as MinimumFileAgeDays. Volumes without deduplication are not listed.
PARAMETER Volume:
Only these volumes. Position 0.
EXAMPLE: Get-DedupVolume
Which volumes save space, and how much.
EXAMPLE: Get-DedupVolume -Volume E: | Format-List *
Every setting of the archive volume.
RELATED: Enable-DedupVolume, Set-DedupVolume, Get-DedupStatus
