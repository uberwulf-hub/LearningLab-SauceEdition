SYNOPSIS: Waits until processes end.
DESCRIPTION:
Wait-Process pauses your script until the named processes have ended, which is useful after Start-Process. With -Timeout it gives up after that many seconds and writes an error for each process still running.
PARAMETER Name:
The process names to wait for. Position 0.
PARAMETER Id:
The process ids to wait for.
PARAMETER InputObject:
Process objects from the pipeline.
PARAMETER Timeout:
The most seconds to wait.
EXAMPLE: Start-Process notepad; Wait-Process -Name notepad -Timeout 30
Waits at most 30 seconds for Notepad to close.
NOTES:
On the practice machine nothing closes programs by itself, so use -Timeout, or end them with Stop-Process.
RELATED: Start-Process, Stop-Process, Get-Process
