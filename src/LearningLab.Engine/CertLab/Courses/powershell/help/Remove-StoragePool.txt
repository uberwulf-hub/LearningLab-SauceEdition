SYNOPSIS: Deletes a storage pool.
DESCRIPTION:
Remove-StoragePool deletes a pool and gives its disks back to the primordial pool. Delete its virtual disks first (Remove-VirtualDisk); a pool that still has some can't be removed. It asks for confirmation.

Afterwards the disks show CanPool True again and can go into a new pool or be initialized as ordinary disks.
PARAMETER FriendlyName:
The pool or pools. Position 0.
EXAMPLE: Get-VirtualDisk | Remove-VirtualDisk -Confirm:$false; Remove-StoragePool -FriendlyName Data -Confirm:$false
Deletes the virtual disks, then the pool.
EXAMPLE: Get-PhysicalDisk -CanPool $true
The freed disks are available again.
RELATED: New-StoragePool, Remove-VirtualDisk, Get-StoragePool
