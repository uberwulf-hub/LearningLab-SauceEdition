SYNOPSIS: Runs a Data Deduplication job now.
DESCRIPTION:
Deduplication works through scheduled background jobs. Start-DedupJob starts one right away:
Optimization deduplicates files (the one that makes savings).
GarbageCollection frees chunks that no file uses any more, after many files were deleted.
Scrubbing checks the chunk store for damage and repairs what it can.
Unoptimization turns optimized files back into normal ones, before turning deduplication off.

-Wait waits until the job finishes; -Full runs a full garbage collection or scrub.
PARAMETER Volume:
The volume or volumes. Position 0.
PARAMETER Type:
Optimization, GarbageCollection, Scrubbing or Unoptimization.
PARAMETER Wait:
Waits for the job to finish.
PARAMETER Full:
A full GarbageCollection or Scrubbing run.
EXAMPLE: Start-DedupJob -Volume E: -Type Optimization -Wait
Optimizes the archive now.
EXAMPLE: Start-DedupJob -Volume E: -Type GarbageCollection -Full
Reclaims space after a large clean-up.
RELATED: Enable-DedupVolume, Get-DedupStatus, Get-DedupVolume, Disable-DedupVolume
