SYNOPSIS: Transfers or seizes operations master (FSMO) roles to another domain controller.
DESCRIPTION:
There are five operations master roles. Per forest: SchemaMaster and DomainNamingMaster. Per domain: PDCEmulator, RIDMaster and InfrastructureMaster.
Move-ADDirectoryServerOperationMasterRole moves roles to the domain controller in -Identity. Normally it's a transfer: the old holder is online and hands the role over cleanly.
With -Force it's a seizure: use it only when the old holder is gone for good, and never bring that server back on the network afterwards.
Roles can be given by name or by number (0 PDCEmulator, 1 RIDMaster, 2 InfrastructureMaster, 3 SchemaMaster, 4 DomainNamingMaster).
PARAMETER Identity:
The domain controller that will hold the roles, such as DC2. Position 0.
PARAMETER OperationMasterRole:
One or more roles. Position 1.
PARAMETER Force:
Seizes the roles when the current holder can't be reached.
PARAMETER PassThru:
Returns the domain controller after the move.
EXAMPLE: Move-ADDirectoryServerOperationMasterRole -Identity DC2 -OperationMasterRole PDCEmulator, RIDMaster
Transfers two domain roles to DC2.
EXAMPLE: Move-ADDirectoryServerOperationMasterRole DC2 0,1,2,3,4 -Force
Seizes all five roles on DC2 after DC1 has failed for good.
RELATED: Get-ADDomain, Get-ADForest, Get-ADDomainController
