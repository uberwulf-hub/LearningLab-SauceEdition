SYNOPSIS: Shows the domain's default password and account lockout policy.
DESCRIPTION:
Every domain has one default password policy, set in the Default Domain Policy GPO and applied to all domain accounts that no fine-grained policy covers. Get-ADDefaultDomainPasswordPolicy shows it: minimum length, history, complexity, minimum and maximum age, and the lockout threshold, duration and observation window.
The Windows Server defaults are 7 characters, 24 remembered passwords, complexity on, a maximum age of 42 days and no lockout (threshold 0).
PARAMETER Identity:
The domain, such as corp.example. Position 0.
PARAMETER Current:
LocalComputer or LoggedOnUser: the domain to read.
PARAMETER Server:
The domain controller to ask.
EXAMPLE: Get-ADDefaultDomainPasswordPolicy
The policy of the current domain.
EXAMPLE: Get-ADDefaultDomainPasswordPolicy | Select-Object MinPasswordLength, LockoutThreshold, LockoutDuration
Just the length and lockout settings.
RELATED: Set-ADDefaultDomainPasswordPolicy, Get-ADFineGrainedPasswordPolicy, Get-ADUserResultantPasswordPolicy
