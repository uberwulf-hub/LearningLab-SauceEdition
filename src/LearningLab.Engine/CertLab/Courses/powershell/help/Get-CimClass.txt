SYNOPSIS: Lists CIM classes and their properties and methods.
DESCRIPTION:
Get-CimClass shows which classes exist in a namespace and what they offer: CimClassProperties (what you can read) and CimClassMethods (what Invoke-CimMethod can run). It is the way to explore before writing a query.
PARAMETER ClassName:
Class names; wildcards are allowed. Position 0.
PARAMETER Namespace:
The namespace; root/cimv2 by default. Position 1.
PARAMETER MethodName:
Only classes with a method of this name.
PARAMETER PropertyName:
Only classes with a property of this name.
EXAMPLE: Get-CimClass -ClassName Win32_*disk*
Classes about disks.
EXAMPLE: (Get-CimClass Win32_Service).CimClassMethods
What you can do to a service through CIM.
RELATED: Get-CimInstance, Invoke-CimMethod
