SYNOPSIS: Changes the schedule of Microsoft Entra Connect Sync.
DESCRIPTION:
Set-ADSyncScheduler turns automatic syncs off and on, or changes the interval. Turning syncs off is the usual step before maintenance or a big change in Active Directory; remember to turn them back on.
The interval can't be shorter than 30 minutes. -NextSyncCyclePolicyType Initial makes the next run a full sync, which is needed after some configuration changes.
PARAMETER SyncCycleEnabled:
$false stops automatic syncs; $true starts them again.
PARAMETER CustomizedSyncCycleInterval:
A new interval, as a TimeSpan such as 01:00:00.
PARAMETER NextSyncCyclePolicyType:
Delta (only changes) or Initial (everything) for the next run.
EXAMPLE: Set-ADSyncScheduler -SyncCycleEnabled $false
Pauses syncing during maintenance.
EXAMPLE: Set-ADSyncScheduler -SyncCycleEnabled $true; Start-ADSyncSyncCycle -PolicyType Delta
Turns it back on and syncs straight away.
RELATED: Get-ADSyncScheduler, Start-ADSyncSyncCycle
