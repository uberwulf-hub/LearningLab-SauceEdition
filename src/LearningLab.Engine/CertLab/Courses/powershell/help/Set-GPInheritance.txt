SYNOPSIS: Blocks or restores Group Policy inheritance on an OU or the domain.
DESCRIPTION:
With inheritance blocked, an OU ignores GPOs linked to its parents and the domain; only its own links apply. Enforced links from above still get through: enforcement beats blocking.
Use blocking sparingly; it makes it harder to see which settings apply where.
PARAMETER Target:
The OU or domain, as a distinguished name. Position 0.
PARAMETER IsBlocked:
Yes to block inheritance, No to restore it. Position 1.
PARAMETER Domain:
The domain, if not your own.
PARAMETER Server:
The domain controller to make the change on.
EXAMPLE: Set-GPInheritance "OU=Kiosks,OU=Workstations,DC=corp,DC=example" -IsBlocked Yes
The kiosks get only the GPOs linked to their own OU (and enforced ones).
EXAMPLE: Set-GPInheritance "OU=Kiosks,OU=Workstations,DC=corp,DC=example" -IsBlocked No
Inheritance back to normal.
RELATED: Get-GPInheritance, Set-GPLink, New-GPLink
