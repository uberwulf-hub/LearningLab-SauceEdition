SYNOPSIS: Lists the targets of a DFS namespace folder.
DESCRIPTION:
Get-DfsnFolderTarget shows each target of a folder with its TargetPath, State (Online or Offline) and ReferralPriorityClass. Offline targets are not handed out to clients.
PARAMETER Path:
The namespace folder. Position 0.
PARAMETER TargetPath:
Only this target.
EXAMPLE: Get-DfsnFolderTarget -Path \\corp.example\Files\Sales
Where users of the Sales folder are sent.
RELATED: New-DfsnFolderTarget, Set-DfsnFolderTarget, Get-DfsnFolder
