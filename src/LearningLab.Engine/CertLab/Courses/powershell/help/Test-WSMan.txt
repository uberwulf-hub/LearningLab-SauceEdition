SYNOPSIS: Checks whether the WinRM service of a computer answers.
DESCRIPTION:
Test-WSMan asks a computer's WinRM service (the service PowerShell remoting uses) to identify itself. An answer means remoting can at least reach the computer; an error usually means WinRM is not running, not listening, or blocked by a firewall.
PARAMETER ComputerName:
The computer to test (default: this one).
PARAMETER Credential:
Another account to connect with.
EXAMPLE: Test-WSMan -ComputerName WIN-CL1
Fails until Enable-PSRemoting has been run on WIN-CL1, then shows the identification.
EXAMPLE: Test-WSMan
Tests this computer.
RELATED: Enable-PSRemoting, Invoke-Command, New-PSSession, about_Remote
