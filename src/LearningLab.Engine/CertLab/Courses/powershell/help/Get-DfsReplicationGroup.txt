SYNOPSIS: Lists DFS Replication groups.
DESCRIPTION:
Get-DfsReplicationGroup shows each replication group with its GroupName, DomainName, Description and State.
PARAMETER GroupName:
Only these groups; wildcards work. Position 0.
EXAMPLE: Get-DfsReplicationGroup
Every replication group in the domain.
EXAMPLE: Get-DfsReplicationGroup -GroupName Sales*
The Sales groups.
RELATED: New-DfsReplicationGroup, Get-DfsrMember, Get-DfsrMembership
