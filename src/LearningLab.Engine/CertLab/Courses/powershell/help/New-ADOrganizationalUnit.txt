SYNOPSIS: Creates an organizational unit (OU) in Active Directory.
DESCRIPTION:
New-ADOrganizationalUnit makes a new OU. Without -Path it is created at the top of the domain (DC=corp,DC=example).
New OUs are protected from accidental deletion by default: Remove-ADOrganizationalUnit refuses until you turn the protection off.
PARAMETER Name:
The OU's name, such as Marketing. Position 0.
PARAMETER Path:
The parent, as a distinguished name, such as "OU=Staff,DC=corp,DC=example".
PARAMETER ProtectedFromAccidentalDeletion:
$true (the default) or $false.
PARAMETER Description:
A note about the OU.
PARAMETER DisplayName:
The display name.
EXAMPLE: New-ADOrganizationalUnit Marketing -Path "OU=Staff,DC=corp,DC=example"
A Marketing OU next to Sales, Finance, HR and IT.
EXAMPLE: New-ADOrganizationalUnit Kiosks -Path "OU=Workstations,DC=corp,DC=example" -Description "Shared PCs in the lobby"
An OU for computers that will get their own Group Policy.
RELATED: Get-ADOrganizationalUnit, Set-ADOrganizationalUnit, Remove-ADOrganizationalUnit, New-GPLink
