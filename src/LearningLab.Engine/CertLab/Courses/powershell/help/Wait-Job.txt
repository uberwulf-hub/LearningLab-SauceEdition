SYNOPSIS: Waits until background jobs have finished.
DESCRIPTION:
Wait-Job blocks until the jobs are no longer running, then outputs the job objects. With -Timeout it gives up after that many seconds.

In Cert Lab, waiting moves the machines' simulated clock on to the moment the jobs finish.
PARAMETER Id:
The job's Id (positional).
PARAMETER Name:
The job's name.
PARAMETER Job:
Job objects (also by the pipeline).
PARAMETER Timeout:
The longest time to wait, in seconds.
EXAMPLE: Start-Job { Start-Sleep 10; 'done' } | Wait-Job
Waits for the job, then shows it with State Completed.
EXAMPLE: Get-Job | Wait-Job | Receive-Job
Waits for all jobs and collects their output.
RELATED: Start-Job, Receive-Job, Get-Job
