SYNOPSIS: Removes a folder from a DFS namespace.
DESCRIPTION:
Remove-DfsnFolder deletes the folder and its targets from the namespace. The shares and files on the servers are not touched; users simply can't reach them through that path any more. Clients that cached the referral may keep using it until their cache times out.
PARAMETER Path:
The folder. Position 0.
PARAMETER Force:
Removes without asking.
EXAMPLE: Remove-DfsnFolder -Path \\corp.example\Files\Sales -Force
The namespace no longer shows Sales; \\FS1\Sales still works.
RELATED: New-DfsnFolder, Get-DfsnFolder
