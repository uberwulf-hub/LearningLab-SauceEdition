SYNOPSIS: Deletes background jobs from the session.
DESCRIPTION:
Finished jobs stay in the session's job list, with any results you have not received, until you remove them or close PowerShell. Remove-Job deletes them, along with their remaining results.

A running job cannot be removed unless you add -Force (which stops it first) or stop it with Stop-Job.
PARAMETER Job:
The job objects to remove. Position 0, and they can come through the pipeline.
PARAMETER Id:
The numbers of the jobs to remove.
PARAMETER Name:
The names of the jobs to remove. Wildcards work.
PARAMETER State:
Removes all jobs in this state, for example -State Completed or -State Failed.
PARAMETER Force:
Removes jobs even while they are running.
EXAMPLE: Get-Job | Remove-Job
Removes every finished job.
EXAMPLE: Remove-Job -State Failed
Removes the jobs that failed.
EXAMPLE: $x = Start-Job { Start-Sleep 30 }; Remove-Job $x
Fails, because the job is still running.
EXAMPLE: Remove-Job $x -Force
Stops and removes it.
NOTES:
The alias is rjb.

Receive the results you need before removing a job; they are gone afterwards.

Receive-Job -Wait -AutoRemoveJob does the receiving and removing in one step.
RELATED: Get-Job, Stop-Job, Start-Job, Receive-Job, about_Jobs
