SYNOPSIS: Shows how much Data Deduplication has saved on each volume.
DESCRIPTION:
Get-DedupStatus shows for each deduplicated volume the FreeSpace, SavedSpace, OptimizedFilesCount and InPolicyFilesCount (files old and big enough to optimize), and when the last jobs ran. Zero savings right after Enable-DedupVolume is normal: nothing is optimized until a job runs.
PARAMETER Volume:
Only these volumes. Position 0.
EXAMPLE: Get-DedupStatus -Volume E:
How much the archive has saved.
EXAMPLE: Get-DedupStatus | Format-List Volume, SavedSpace, OptimizedFilesCount, LastOptimizationTime
The main figures for every volume.
RELATED: Get-DedupVolume, Start-DedupJob, Enable-DedupVolume
