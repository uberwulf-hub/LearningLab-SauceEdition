SYNOPSIS: Shows information about an Active Directory forest.
DESCRIPTION:
Get-ADForest returns the forest root domain, every domain in the forest, the sites, the global catalog servers, the forest functional level (ForestMode), the extra UPN suffixes, and the two forest-wide operations master roles: SchemaMaster and DomainNamingMaster.
PARAMETER Identity:
The forest, by its root domain name, such as corp.example. Position 0.
PARAMETER Current:
LocalComputer or LoggedOnUser.
PARAMETER Server:
The domain controller to ask.
EXAMPLE: Get-ADForest | Format-List Name, ForestMode, SchemaMaster, DomainNamingMaster
The forest's level and its two forest-wide role holders.
EXAMPLE: (Get-ADForest).GlobalCatalogs
The domain controllers that are global catalog servers.
RELATED: Get-ADDomain, Set-ADForest, Get-ADDomainController, Get-ADReplicationSite
