SYNOPSIS: Deletes a DNS query resolution policy.
DESCRIPTION:
Remove-DnsServerQueryResolutionPolicy deletes a policy by name. For a zone-level policy give -ZoneName too, otherwise the server-level list is searched. The client subnets and zone scopes the policy used stay; remove them separately if nothing else needs them.
PARAMETER Name:
The policy to delete.
PARAMETER ZoneName:
The zone the policy belongs to.
PARAMETER Force:
Deletes without asking.
EXAMPLE: Remove-DnsServerQueryResolutionPolicy -Name BranchPolicy -ZoneName corp.example -Force -ComputerName DC1
Branch clients get the default answers again.
RELATED: Get-DnsServerQueryResolutionPolicy, Add-DnsServerQueryResolutionPolicy
