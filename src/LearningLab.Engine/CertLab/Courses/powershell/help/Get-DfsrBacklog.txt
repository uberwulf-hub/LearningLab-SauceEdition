SYNOPSIS: Lists the files still waiting to replicate from one member to another.
DESCRIPTION:
Get-DfsrBacklog compares two members and lists the files the source has that the destination hasn't received yet. An empty result means they are in step. A backlog that keeps growing points to a problem: a stopped connection, a full staging area, or a member that hasn't picked up its settings.

Pipe it to Measure-Object to just count the files.
PARAMETER GroupName:
The replication group.
PARAMETER FolderName:
The replicated folder.
PARAMETER SourceComputerName:
The member that sends.
PARAMETER DestinationComputerName:
The member that receives.
EXAMPLE: Get-DfsrBacklog -GroupName Sales-Replication -FolderName Sales -SourceComputerName FS1 -DestinationComputerName SRV1
The Sales files SRV1 doesn't have yet.
EXAMPLE: (Get-DfsrBacklog -GroupName Sales-Replication -FolderName Sales -SourceComputerName FS1 -DestinationComputerName SRV1 | Measure-Object).Count
How many there are.
RELATED: Get-DfsrMembership, Update-DfsrConfigurationFromAD, Set-DfsrMembership
