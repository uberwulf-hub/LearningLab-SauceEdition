SYNOPSIS: Lists Active Directory optional features, such as the Recycle Bin, and whether they are on.
DESCRIPTION:
Optional features are forest-wide switches. The best known is the Recycle Bin Feature. A feature is turned on where its EnabledScopes property lists something; an empty EnabledScopes means it is off.
PARAMETER Identity:
One feature, such as "Recycle Bin Feature". Position 0.
PARAMETER Filter:
A search, such as *.
PARAMETER Server:
The domain controller to ask.
EXAMPLE: Get-ADOptionalFeature -Filter * | Select-Object Name, EnabledScopes
Every optional feature and where it is on.
EXAMPLE: Get-ADOptionalFeature "Recycle Bin Feature"
Whether the Recycle Bin is on.
RELATED: Enable-ADOptionalFeature, Restore-ADObject, Get-ADForest
