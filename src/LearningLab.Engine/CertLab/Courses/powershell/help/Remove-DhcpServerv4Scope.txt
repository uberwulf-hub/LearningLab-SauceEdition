SYNOPSIS: Deletes an IPv4 scope with its leases, reservations and options.
DESCRIPTION:
Remove-DhcpServerv4Scope deletes the scope and everything in it. Clients keep their addresses until the lease runs out, then can't renew. It asks for confirmation; -Force skips it.

A scope that is part of a failover relationship should be taken out of it first.
PARAMETER ScopeId:
The scope or scopes to delete.
PARAMETER Force:
Deletes without asking.
EXAMPLE: Remove-DhcpServerv4Scope -ScopeId 10.20.0.0 -ComputerName SRV1
Deletes the branch scope after you confirm.
RELATED: Get-DhcpServerv4Scope, Add-DhcpServerv4Scope, Remove-DhcpServerv4Failover
