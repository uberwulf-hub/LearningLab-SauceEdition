SYNOPSIS: Adds a folder to a DFS namespace that points to a share.
DESCRIPTION:
New-DfsnFolder adds a folder under a namespace, with its first target: the share users reach when they open it. More targets (for example a copy on another server, kept in step by DFS Replication) are added with New-DfsnFolderTarget.

Users browsing \\corp.example\Files\Sales are sent to \\FS1\Sales without seeing the server name.
PARAMETER Path:
The folder's path in the namespace, for example \\corp.example\Files\Sales.
PARAMETER TargetPath:
The share it leads to, for example \\FS1\Sales.
PARAMETER Description:
A description.
PARAMETER State:
Online (default) or Offline.
EXAMPLE: New-DfsnFolder -Path \\corp.example\Files\Sales -TargetPath \\FS1\Sales
\\corp.example\Files\Sales now leads to the Sales share on FS1.
EXAMPLE: Get-DfsnFolder -Path "\\corp.example\Files\*"
Lists the namespace's folders.
RELATED: Get-DfsnFolder, New-DfsnFolderTarget, Remove-DfsnFolder, New-DfsnRoot
