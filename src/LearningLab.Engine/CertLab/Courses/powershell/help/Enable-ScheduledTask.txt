SYNOPSIS: Turns a disabled scheduled task back on.
DESCRIPTION:
Enable-ScheduledTask sets a disabled task to Ready, so its triggers work again, and outputs the task.
PARAMETER TaskName:
The task. Position 0.
PARAMETER TaskPath:
Its folder. Position 1.
PARAMETER InputObject:
Task objects from the pipeline.
EXAMPLE: Enable-ScheduledTask -TaskName Nightly
RELATED: Disable-ScheduledTask
