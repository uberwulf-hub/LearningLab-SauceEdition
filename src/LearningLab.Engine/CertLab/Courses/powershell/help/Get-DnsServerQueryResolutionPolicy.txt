SYNOPSIS: Lists the DNS policies that control how questions are answered.
DESCRIPTION:
Get-DnsServerQueryResolutionPolicy shows the query resolution policies with their action, conditions, processing order and whether they are enabled. Without -ZoneName it shows the server-level policies; with it, the policies of that zone.
PARAMETER Name:
Shows only this policy.
PARAMETER ZoneName:
Shows the policies of this zone.
EXAMPLE: Get-DnsServerQueryResolutionPolicy -ZoneName corp.example -ComputerName DC1
The zone-level policies of the company zone.
EXAMPLE: Get-DnsServerQueryResolutionPolicy -ComputerName DC1
The server-level policies, such as blocks.
RELATED: Add-DnsServerQueryResolutionPolicy, Remove-DnsServerQueryResolutionPolicy, Get-DnsServerClientSubnet, Get-DnsServerZoneScope
