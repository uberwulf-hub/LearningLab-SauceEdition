SYNOPSIS: Lists the zone scopes of a zone.
DESCRIPTION:
Get-DnsServerZoneScope shows the scopes of one zone. Every zone has a default scope with the zone's own name; the others were added with Add-DnsServerZoneScope for DNS policies.
PARAMETER ZoneName:
The zone to look at.
EXAMPLE: Get-DnsServerZoneScope -ZoneName corp.example -ComputerName DC1
Lists the default scope and any added ones such as BranchScope.
EXAMPLE: Get-DnsServerResourceRecord corp.example -ZoneScope BranchScope
Shows the records inside one scope.
RELATED: Add-DnsServerZoneScope, Get-DnsServerResourceRecord, Get-DnsServerQueryResolutionPolicy
