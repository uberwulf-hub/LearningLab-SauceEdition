SYNOPSIS: Deletes a Group Policy Object.
DESCRIPTION:
Remove-GPO deletes the GPO and, by default, all its links, so the settings stop applying at the next refresh. To stop a GPO from applying without losing it, remove or disable its link instead (Remove-GPLink, Set-GPLink -LinkEnabled No).
Take a backup first (Backup-GPO): a deleted GPO can only come back from a backup.
PARAMETER Name:
The GPO's display name. Position 0.
PARAMETER Guid:
The GPO's GUID.
PARAMETER KeepLinks:
Leaves the links in place (they then point at nothing).
PARAMETER Domain:
The domain, if not your own.
PARAMETER Server:
The domain controller to make the change on.
EXAMPLE: Backup-GPO "Sales Desktop" -Path C:\GPOBackups; Remove-GPO "Sales Desktop"
Backs up a GPO and then deletes it.
EXAMPLE: Get-GPO -All | Where-Object DisplayName -like "Test*" | ForEach-Object { Remove-GPO -Guid $_.Id }
Deletes every GPO whose name starts with Test.
RELATED: Get-GPO, Backup-GPO, Restore-GPO, Remove-GPLink
