SYNOPSIS: Removes members from an Active Directory group.
DESCRIPTION:
Remove-ADGroupMember takes accounts or groups out of one group. It asks for confirmation unless you add -Confirm:$false.
People keep the old membership in their access token until they sign out and back in.
PARAMETER Identity:
The group. Position 0.
PARAMETER Members:
The members to remove. Position 1.
PARAMETER PassThru:
Returns the group after the change.
EXAMPLE: Remove-ADGroupMember "Sales Managers" -Members imoreau -Confirm:$false
Takes Ines Moreau out of Sales Managers without asking.
EXAMPLE: Get-ADGroupMember Helpdesk | Where-Object Name -ne "Jamal Price" | ForEach-Object { Remove-ADGroupMember Helpdesk -Members $_ }
Empties the Helpdesk group except for Jamal Price.
RELATED: Add-ADGroupMember, Get-ADGroupMember
