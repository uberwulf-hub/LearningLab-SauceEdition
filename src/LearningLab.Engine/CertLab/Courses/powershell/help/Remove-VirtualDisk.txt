SYNOPSIS: Deletes a virtual disk (storage space) and all its data.
DESCRIPTION:
Remove-VirtualDisk deletes the storage space and returns its space to the pool. Every volume and file on it is gone. It asks for confirmation.

Check which drive letter it carries before deleting: Get-VirtualDisk -FriendlyName Mirror1 | Get-Disk | Get-Partition.
PARAMETER FriendlyName:
The virtual disk or disks. Position 0.
EXAMPLE: Remove-VirtualDisk -FriendlyName Mirror1
Deletes Mirror1 after you confirm.
EXAMPLE: Get-VirtualDisk | Remove-VirtualDisk -Confirm:$false
Deletes every virtual disk without asking, for example before removing the pool.
RELATED: New-VirtualDisk, Get-VirtualDisk, Remove-StoragePool
