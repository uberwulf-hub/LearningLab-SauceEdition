SYNOPSIS: Deletes records from a DNS zone.
DESCRIPTION:
Remove-DnsServerResourceRecord deletes the records with a given name and type. When a name has several records of that type (round robin A records, two MX records), add -RecordData to remove just one of them.

You can also pipe records from Get-DnsServerResourceRecord. It asks for confirmation; -Force skips that.

A computer that registers itself dynamically will add its A record again at its next registration (every 24 hours, or at ipconfig /registerdns).
PARAMETER ZoneName:
The zone.
PARAMETER Name:
The record name, for example app.
PARAMETER RRType:
The record type: A, AAAA, CName, MX, NS, Ptr, Srv or Txt.
PARAMETER RecordData:
The data of the one record to remove, for example 10.10.0.60.
PARAMETER InputObject:
Records piped in from Get-DnsServerResourceRecord.
PARAMETER Force:
Deletes without asking.
EXAMPLE: Remove-DnsServerResourceRecord -ZoneName corp.example -Name app -RRType A -RecordData 10.10.0.60 -Force
Removes that one address from app.corp.example.
EXAMPLE: Get-DnsServerResourceRecord corp.example -Name wiki | Remove-DnsServerResourceRecord -ZoneName corp.example -Force
Removes the wiki alias found by Get-DnsServerResourceRecord.
RELATED: Get-DnsServerResourceRecord, Add-DnsServerResourceRecordA, Add-DnsServerResourceRecord
