SYNOPSIS: Lists fine-grained password policies.
DESCRIPTION:
Get-ADFineGrainedPasswordPolicy returns password settings objects with their precedence, settings and the users and groups they apply to (AppliesTo). They live in the Password Settings Container under CN=System.
PARAMETER Identity:
One policy, by name. Position 0, and takes pipeline input.
PARAMETER Filter:
A search, such as *.
PARAMETER Server:
The domain controller to ask.
EXAMPLE: Get-ADFineGrainedPasswordPolicy -Filter * | Select-Object Name, Precedence, MinPasswordLength, AppliesTo
Every policy, its precedence and who it covers.
EXAMPLE: Get-ADFineGrainedPasswordPolicy AdminsPSO
One policy in full.
RELATED: New-ADFineGrainedPasswordPolicy, Add-ADFineGrainedPasswordPolicySubject, Get-ADUserResultantPasswordPolicy
