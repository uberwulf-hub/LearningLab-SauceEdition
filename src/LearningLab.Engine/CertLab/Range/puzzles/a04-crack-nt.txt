@intro
~/sam.txt is an export of Windows-style account hashes, one per line, in the form
USER:RID:LM:NT::: The part that matters is the NT hash (the fourth field). Tell
hashcrack it is an nt export and crack the user "svc" password. (These are Cyber
Range's own simulated hashes.)

@setup
printf 'guest:501:aad3b435b51404eeaad3b435b51404ee:%s:::\n' '{{SIMHASH:SECRET}}' > /home/player/sam.txt
printf 'svc:1104:aad3b435b51404eeaad3b435b51404ee:%s:::\n' '{{SIMHASH:SECRET}}' >> /home/player/sam.txt
chown player: /home/player/sam.txt

@solution
cat sam.txt
hashcrack --format=nt sam.txt

@walkthrough
Each line is USER:RID:LM:NT::: hashcrack reads the NT field (the fourth) and tries
the word list against it:

    hashcrack --format=nt sam.txt

In the range these are a simulated NT format, but the idea is real: weak Windows
passwords fall to a word list, and a captured hash is enough to try (no need to log
in each time). Long passphrases defeat it.

@hint 10
hashcrack reads the USER:RID:LM:NT::: format when you say --format=nt.

@hint 14
Run: hashcrack --format=nt sam.txt
