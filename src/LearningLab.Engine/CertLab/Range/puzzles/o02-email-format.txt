var: CO = company
var: CFO = person
var: OTHER = person
var: FMT = pick:first.last|last.first|first_last|firstlast

@vars FMT=first.last
EMAIL = {{LOWER:CFO_FIRST}}.{{LOWER:CFO_LAST}}@{{CO_DOMAIN}}
EXAMPLE = {{LOWER:OTHER_FIRST}}.{{LOWER:OTHER_LAST}}@{{CO_DOMAIN}}
@vars FMT=last.first
EMAIL = {{LOWER:CFO_LAST}}.{{LOWER:CFO_FIRST}}@{{CO_DOMAIN}}
EXAMPLE = {{LOWER:OTHER_LAST}}.{{LOWER:OTHER_FIRST}}@{{CO_DOMAIN}}
@vars FMT=first_last
EMAIL = {{LOWER:CFO_FIRST}}_{{LOWER:CFO_LAST}}@{{CO_DOMAIN}}
EXAMPLE = {{LOWER:OTHER_FIRST}}_{{LOWER:OTHER_LAST}}@{{CO_DOMAIN}}
@vars FMT=firstlast
EMAIL = {{LOWER:CFO_FIRST}}{{LOWER:CFO_LAST}}@{{CO_DOMAIN}}
EXAMPLE = {{LOWER:OTHER_FIRST}}{{LOWER:OTHER_LAST}}@{{CO_DOMAIN}}

@intro
Attackers sending a fake invoice want the finance chief's e-mail address. In ~/osint are
two pages saved from {{CO}}'s (made-up) web site. Neither lists that address, but
together they give it away. Work it out. (Everyone here is invented.)

@setup
mkdir -p /home/player/osint
cat > /home/player/osint/team.html <<HTML
<h1>Meet the team at {{CO}}</h1>
<ul>
<li>{{OTHER_FULL}} - Head of People</li>
<li>{{CFO_FULL}} - Chief Financial Officer</li>
<li>Robin Vale - Warehouse lead</li>
</ul>
HTML
cat > /home/player/osint/jobs.html <<HTML
<h2>We're hiring: accounts assistant</h2>
<p>Send your CV and a short letter to {{OTHER_FIRST}} in People: {{EXAMPLE}}</p>
HTML
chown -R player: /home/player/osint

@solution
cat osint/team.html
cat osint/jobs.html
echo {{EMAIL}}

@walkthrough
1. team.html names the finance chief: {{CFO_FULL}}.
2. jobs.html shows one real address, {{EXAMPLE}}, for {{OTHER_FULL}}. That reveals the
   company's pattern (here {{FMT}}, lower case) and its domain, {{CO_DOMAIN}}.
3. Apply the pattern to the CFO: {{EMAIL}}.

This is why phishing works so well: a name on a team page plus one published address is
enough. Defences: expect the pattern to be known, and train finance staff to confirm
payment requests another way.

@hint 15
One page has the name you need; the other shows how this company builds its e-mail addresses.

@hint 25
Copy the pattern of the address in jobs.html (lower case, same separator, same domain) using the CFO's first and last names.
