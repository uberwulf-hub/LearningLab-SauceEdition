@intro
~/stack.txt went through three steps: the flag was reversed, then ROT13'd, then
base64-encoded. Undo them in the opposite order (base64 decode, then ROT13, then
reverse) to read the flag.

@setup
printf '%s' '{{FLAG}}' | rev | tr 'A-Za-z' 'N-ZA-Mn-za-m' | base64 > /home/player/stack.txt
chown player: /home/player/stack.txt

@solution
cat stack.txt
base64 -d stack.txt
base64 -d stack.txt | tr 'A-Za-z' 'N-ZA-Mn-za-m'
base64 -d stack.txt | tr 'A-Za-z' 'N-ZA-Mn-za-m' | rev
echo

@walkthrough
To undo a stack of changes, reverse each step in the opposite order. The file is
base64 on the outside, so decode that first; the result is ROT13, so step the letters
13 places; that result is backwards, so reverse it:

    base64 -d stack.txt | tr 'A-Za-z' 'N-ZA-Mn-za-m' | rev

@hint 10
Work from the outside in: base64 first (the text looks like base64), then ROT13,
then reverse.
