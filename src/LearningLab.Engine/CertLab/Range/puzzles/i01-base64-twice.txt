title: Wrapped twice
level: Intermediate
kind: Decoding
points: 50
order: 10
answer: flag

@intro
~/data.txt was base64-encoded, and then that result was base64-encoded again. Peel
both layers to reach the flag.

@setup
printf '%s' '{{FLAG}}' | base64 | base64 > /home/player/data.txt
chown player: /home/player/data.txt

@solution
cat data.txt
base64 -d data.txt
base64 -d data.txt | base64 -d
echo

@walkthrough
Decoding once gives you... more base64. That is the clue to decode again. You can
chain the two decodes in a pipe:

    base64 -d data.txt | base64 -d

@hint 4
Think in layers: whatever you decode, look at the result and ask "what does this look like?" before you stop.

@hint 8
Decode it once (base64 -d data.txt) and look carefully at the result: it is base64 again - letters, digits, + and /, maybe = at the end.

@hint 12
The | pipe hands one command's output to the next, so both layers come off at once: base64 -d data.txt | base64 -d
