answer: flag
var: KW = word
var: K = num:1-255
var: SHIP = pick:the Petrel|the Wren|the Gannet|the Kestrel|the Heron

@intro
~/locked.txt was locked three times. The logbook ~/logbook.txt may help with the last
lock.

@setup
printf '%s' '{{VIG:KW:FLAG}}' | xorcat -e -k {{K}} | base32 > /home/player/locked.txt
printf 'Logbook of {{SHIP}}\nDay 3: calm seas.\nDay 4: the captain changed the word to "{{KW}}". Byte stays the same: 0x%x.\n' {{K}} > /home/player/logbook.txt
chown player: /home/player/locked.txt /home/player/logbook.txt

@solution
cat logbook.txt
base32 -d locked.txt | xorcat -k {{K}} | vigenere -d -k {{KW}}

@walkthrough
Peel the locks from the outside in:

1. locked.txt uses only A-Z, 2-7 and =: base32. base32 -d gives hex digits.
2. Hex plus "the byte" in the logbook: a single-byte XOR with key {{K}} (xorcat -k).
   The result has the shape of a flag, but the letters are wrong.
3. Letters wrong, punctuation kept: a letter cipher with "the word" {{KW}}:
   vigenere -d -k {{KW}}.

    base32 -d locked.txt | xorcat -k {{K}} | vigenere -d -k {{KW}}

@hint 30
Outside in: base32, then one byte of XOR, then a Vigenere word. The logbook gives the byte and the word.
