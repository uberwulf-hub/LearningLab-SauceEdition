gives addresses out again as people come and go. ~/vpn.log and ~/firewall.log are your
evidence. Which user was it?

@solution

@walkthrough
The address 10.8.0.{{LAST}} belonged to three different sessions that day:

- {{U1}} from 20:02 to 21:58
- {{U2}} from 22:09 to 22:55
- {{U1}} again from 23:10

The upload at 22:41:19 falls inside {{U2}}'s session, so {{U2}} is the answer - not
{{U1}}, whose name appears most. An address is only an identity together with a time.
