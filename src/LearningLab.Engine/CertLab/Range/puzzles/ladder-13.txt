@intro
You are step13. secret.txt looks like nonsense, but every letter in it has been
moved 13 places along the alphabet (a becomes n, b becomes o... and after z it starts
again at a). Digits were left alone. This old trick is called ROT13. Undo it.

@setup
useradd -m step14
echo 'step14:{{PW}}' | chpasswd
printf '%s\n' '{{ROT13:LINE}}' > /home/step13/secret.txt
chown step13: /home/step13/secret.txt

@solution
cat secret.txt
cat secret.txt | tr 'A-Za-z' 'N-ZA-Mn-za-m'

@walkthrough
tr ("translate") swaps characters: every character in the first list becomes the
matching character in the second list.

    cat secret.txt | tr 'A-Za-z' 'N-ZA-Mn-za-m'

The first list is the alphabet A to Z then a to z; the second starts at N, wraps
round to M, and does the same for lower case, so each letter moves 13 places. Because
the alphabet has 26 letters, moving 13 twice brings you back where you started: the
same command both hides and reveals ROT13 text.

@hint 10
The tr command changes characters: tr 'abc' 'xyz' turns every a into x, b into y and c into z. You want every letter replaced by the letter 13 places further on, wrapping around after z. The sentence starts with "Gur", which is "The" moved 13 places.

@hint 18
cat secret.txt | tr 'A-Za-z' 'N-ZA-Mn-za-m' - the two lists line up A with N, ..., M with Z, N with A, and the same for small letters.
